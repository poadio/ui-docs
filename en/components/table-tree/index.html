What it is for

For a hierarchy of rows of the same shape (projects, environments, services; folders and files). Children are in a field of the row (treeField) or point to their parent’s key in flat data (parentKey with dataKey). The toggle and the indent go in the first column, or the treeColumn one. Expanded nodes (expandedNodeKeys) can be held by the application. A filter keeps the ancestors of a matching row and opens them (keepAncestors; otherwise matching rows come up flat). selectionPropagation sets how selection spreads: down, up, both (default) or none. With lazyChildren, a row without known children (unless leaf: true) has a button; opening it calls loadChildren, and a spinner turns while loading. Pagination counts the roots. For categories computed from a column, see row groups.

Keyboard

→Expands the node; if it already is, moves to its first child.
←Collapses the node; if it already is (or has no children), moves to its parent.

↓ ↑Next / previous row in display order.

Enter SpaceWith a selection: selects or clears the active row (and its descendants, by propagation); on a toggle button reached with the mouse: expands or collapses it.

Accessibility

  • The tree table is a treegrid: each row carries aria-level, aria-posinset, aria-setsize and, when it has children, aria-expanded.
  • The toggle button is named “Expand” / “Collapse” by the catalog and carries aria-expanded; it stays, hidden, in a leaf row to keep the alignment.
  • With propagation, a parent’s checkbox goes to the mixed state (aria-checked="mixed") when some of its descendants are selected.

What it is for

To read a list by category (environment, owner, status) and compare sums or averages. groupBy takes a field, a function of the row or a list (one level each); grouped columns are hidden (hideGroupedColumns). aggregates maps a field to sum, avg, min, max, count or a function of the rows. Groups start open; expandedGroups (keys of the open groups) can be held by the application. Pagination counts the top-level groups. The table panel provides the Groups and Values tabs to change grouping and aggregates without code: in the Groups tab, each level is a chip you reorder (with the mouse by its handle, or from the keyboard: Space, ← →, Space) and remove (Delete, Backspace or ×). The demo opens it to the right of the table.

Accessibility

  • The grouped table is a treegrid: each group header carries aria-level, aria-expanded, aria-posinset and aria-setsize, its rows are one level lower.
  • A group’s button is named “Expand” / “Collapse”, its checkbox “Select group <label>”; it goes to the mixed state when some of its rows are selected.
  • In the panel’s Groups tab, each chip has a <button> handle named “Group Env”, described by the keyboard help; grabbing, moving, dropping and removing are said in an aria-live region (“Group Env grabbed, position 1 of 2”, “No longer grouped by Env”).
  • A group without a value takes the catalog label (“(empty)”), in the page’s language.

What it is for

To let users explore their data by category (environment, team…) without a settings screen. showGroupZone shows the zone and puts a handle on every header: dropped on the zone, it groups by its column. From the keyboard, the column menu’s “Group by” (columnMenu) does the same. Chips are reordered (first level on the left) and removed with Delete or their ×. A column without a field or with groupable: false does not group. The table keeps the grouping; control it to keep it: groupBy / onGroupByChange in React, v-model:group-by in Vue, [(groupBy)] in Angular, the web component’s groupBy property. The groups themselves (folding, aggregates, footers) are those of the “Row groups” page. In cards, no zone.

How to change

In the application’s global stylesheet, after the @poadio/ui-core sheets: on :root for the whole application, or on a container for one area only. A floating layer (menu, list, dialog) may be rendered outside its container: for it, prefer :root.

/* The whole application */
:root {
  --poad-accent: #0b7a5c;        /* Accent colour: primary button, checked box, selection */

}

.theme-dark {
  --poad-accent: #3fcf9f;        /* Accent colour: primary button, checked box, selection */
